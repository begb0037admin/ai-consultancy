# AI Support Operations Consultancy

> I help small businesses fix their support operations — IT helpdesk and call handling — by implementing and configuring the right AI tools properly, instead of leaving them broken or over-buying enterprise software they cannot run.

This is the working repository for launching a UK-based AI implementation consultancy for small and medium-sized businesses. It is both a checkable launch plan and a place to record what changes as the offer is tested with real prospects.

The positioning above is a first draft. It is the single source of truth for the one-line offer and should change when discovery conversations reveal clearer customer language.

## The model

The consultancy is the practical middle layer between an SMB and the crowded AI software market:

- assess where AI will improve support and where it could damage the customer experience;
- select suitable existing helpdesk, knowledge-base, call-handling, and reporting tools;
- configure routing, knowledge lookup, escalation, quality controls, and visibility properly;
- implement the chosen tools and help the team operate them after launch.

The aim is not to build another general-purpose AI platform. The value is informed tool selection, hands-on implementation, and knowing when a human should stay in the loop.

## Initial proof

Two public demos will use entirely synthetic company data:

1. a searchable support knowledge base;
2. a support-triage dashboard showing workload, routing, escalation, and service health.

They will demonstrate the underlying implementation skills without exposing any former employer's systems, branding, data, or screenshots.

## Guardrails

- Use synthetic data and fictional organisations in every public demo.
- Do not publish former-employer system names, data, branding, credentials, or screenshots.
- Prefer configuring proven vendor tools to custom development unless a small integration is genuinely necessary.
- Delay pricing decisions until discovery conversations provide evidence about value and budget.
