## Decision log

### July 2026 — Avoid generic AI-consultant positioning

“AI consultant for SMBs” is too broad and does not distinguish the offer from general ChatGPT advice. The business needs a narrower operational problem and a credible implementation angle.

### July 2026 — Do not sell AEO as the first service

Answer Engine Optimisation is a crowded agency category with a long sales cycle, making it a poor first offer for a new consultant serving SMB budgets. AEO may still be useful for the consultancy's own site.

### July 2026 — Use support operations as the umbrella

IT helpdesk, first-line support, and call-centre triage share the same core work: routing, knowledge lookup, escalation logic, and operational visibility. “Support operations” covers both channels without limiting the business to IT alone.

### July 2026 — Rebuild prior work as public synthetic-data demos

The founder has relevant experience building a searchable support knowledge base and a live support-ticket dashboard. Public demos will reproduce those problem-solving patterns using fictional organisations and synthetic data only. They will not include former-employer data, branding, internal system names, or screenshots.

### July 2026 — Adopt the middle-layer implementation model

The tooling market already contains capable, well-funded platforms. The consultancy will select, configure, integrate, and operationalise existing tools instead of competing by building another platform. Its differentiation is judgment: knowing where AI helps, where it hurts, and how to deploy it with appropriate escalation and visibility.

### July 2026 — Run outreach before the assets are finished

The launch has five phases, but discovery is not the final step. Initial outreach begins in parallel with demo and website work so prospect language and priorities can shape the offer early.
